How it works

The C.A.S. Method

Every engagement follows the same three-step arc, so you always know what to expect and leave with something real.

C

Calibrate

We start by getting aligned. Where are you now, what's blocking you, and what would a win look like? No assumptions, just a clear read of your situation.

Common questions

Good to know before we talk

Do I need to be technical to work with you?

Not at all. Most clients aren't. I handle the technical side and translate everything into plain language and clear next steps. You bring the business problem; I bring the AI.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Individuals, solo operators, and small teams who want to use AI well without hiring a full data team. If you have a repetitive workflow or a decision you keep making, there's usually something to automate or accelerate.

How fast can we get started?

Most new inquiries get a reply within 24 hours, and sessions can usually be booked within the same week. There's no lengthy onboarding before the work begins.

Will I actually leave with something usable?

Yes. That's the whole point of the "Ship" step. Before a session ends, you'll have used at least one tool hands-on and have something running you can keep using.

Ready to put AI to work?

Start with one focused session. If it's useful, we go from there. No retainers, no pressure.
