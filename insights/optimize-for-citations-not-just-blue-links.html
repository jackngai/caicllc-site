Optimize for Citations, Not Just Blue Links

AI SEO moves the goalpost from ranking for keywords to becoming the source of truth that LLMs cite in their answers.

September 22, 2026

  1. Optimize for Prompt Intent

    Stop targeting isolated keywords and start targeting natural language prompts. AI models look for content that directly answers complex user questions. Map out the exact phrasing your customers use in chat interfaces to guide your content strategy.

  2. Focus on Mention Gaps

    Identify prompts where your competitors are mentioned in LLM outputs but your brand is absent. These are your biggest opportunities for new content. Write pages that provide better or more current data than the sources the models are currently citing.

  3. Implement a Site Guidance File

    Use an llm.txt file to help AI models crawl and interpret your site more efficiently. This file serves as a map for LLMs, highlighting the most important data and giving clear permission for scraping. It reduces the friction for an AI to include your site in its responses.

  4. Monitor Brand Visibility Metrics

    Track how often your brand appears in AI responses and what the surrounding sentiment looks like. Traditional rank tracking misses the context of how an AI describes your business. Shift your reporting to focus on your share of citations within your industry.

Why it matters

Small businesses often lose out to big brands in traditional search volume battles. AI SEO levels the field by rewarding specific, high quality answers over domain authority. Builders who optimize early for LLM citations can capture high intent traffic before the market becomes saturated.
