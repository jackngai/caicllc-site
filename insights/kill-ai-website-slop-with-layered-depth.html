Kill AI Website Slop with Layered Depth

Stop accepting generic AI web designs by using external reference libraries and a specific layering framework.

September 11, 2026

  1. Build UI depth through layering

    Create a premium feel by explicitly prompting for distinct planes of interaction. Tell the AI to separate the background, the subject, and the typography into different layers. This technique, often called scroll craft, produces a sophisticated parallax effect that distinguishes your site from flat, generic templates.

  2. Use reference libraries as steering wheels

    Provide the model with specific code snippets or component links from platforms like 21st.dev or Godly. Giving the AI a concrete starting point prevents it from falling back on its average training data. You can refine individual elements by asking the model to swap a default button with a specific style found in an inspiration gallery.

  3. Apply the Three Ps framework

    Align your design complexity with the Pain, Persona, and Proposition of your business. A 3D immersive world might work for a luxury brand or a young audience, but it can frustrate a professional looking for quick information. Use the AI to match the aesthetic intensity to the specific user journey you are trying to facilitate.

  4. Iterate with brand guidelines and copy

    Treat the first generation as a rough draft rather than a final product. Feed your specific brand book and actual marketing copy into the model to refine the output. High quality results come from providing the AI with the creative constraints it needs to stay on brand during the second and third passes.

Why it matters

Small businesses often suffer from websites that look cheap or clearly AI generated. These techniques allow a solo builder to produce agency level immersive designs without a professional budget. It ensures that your technology use enhances your brand rather than making it look like low quality slop.
