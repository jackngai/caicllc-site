Run Your Business Operations Through a Claude Master Session

Stop wasting time switching between dozens of active chats by turning one session into a central command hub for your entire project.

October 30, 2026

  1. Centralize orchestration with Master Sessions

    Pin one session to act as your manager and instruct it to monitor your other active threads. It can summarize blockers and highlight decisions across up to twenty chats, effectively acting as an executive assistant for your AI workflows.

  2. Swap sub-agents for Nested Sessions

    Sub-agents are often black boxes that provide a single result. Nested sessions create distinct chats you can actually enter, allowing you to fine-tune specific sub-tasks like marketing copy or code snippets before they report back to the main thread.

  3. Deploy AI-powered tools to your team

    Use the AI-powered artifacts setting to embed interactive chat windows directly into shared dashboards and documents. This lets team members query your project data in plain English without them ever needing to touch the Claude Code interface.

  4. Use cloud persistence for long-running builds

    Standard Claude sessions run locally and stop when your computer sleeps. By moving work into Projects, you ensure tasks continue running on Anthropic servers even when your device is turned off.

Why it matters

For small businesses, the overhead of managing multiple AI outputs often eats the time savings. These tools shift Claude from a simple chat box to an operating system that runs background tasks while you focus on high-level decisions.
