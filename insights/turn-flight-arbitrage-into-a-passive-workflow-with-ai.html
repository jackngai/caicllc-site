Turn Flight Arbitrage into a Passive Workflow with AI

You can fly business class for 70 percent off by using an AI agent to monitor airline point sales and live award inventory.

November 8, 2026

  1. Target Point Arbitrage Over Loyalty

    Instead of earning points slowly through credit card spend, buy them directly during massive bonus sales from programs like Aeroplan or LifeMiles. When these airlines offer 80 to 90 percent bonuses, the effective cost of a business class seat drops to the price of an economy ticket. This strategy relies on capital efficiency rather than long term brand loyalty.

  2. Verify Inventory Before Moving Capital

    Point transfers and purchases are often non-refundable, making it risky to buy points without a confirmed seat. Use seats.aero with the green checked filter to find real time availability for business class cabins. Always check the specific number of seats available to ensure your entire party can fit before you commit to a points purchase.

  3. Automate Deal Math with Visual AI

    Calculating the value of a points deal involves complex math across different currencies and bonus tiers. Feed screenshots of the point sale and the cash price from Skyscanner into Claude to determine the deal floor. The AI can instantly calculate if the points price is at least 60 percent lower than the cash price, which is the benchmark for a high value booking.

  4. Deploy a Monitoring Prompt

    Build a workflow where Claude scans loyalty program updates and seat alerts to send you a daily summary. This removes the need to manually check multiple airline websites every morning. By setting specific parameters for routes and dates in your prompt, you only receive notifications when a flight meets your exact business travel requirements.

Why it matters

Travel is a significant overhead for small businesses and solo builders. Automating the discovery of heavily discounted premium flights saves thousands of dollars and dozens of hours in manual research, allowing you to reinvest that capital and time back into your product.
