Build interactive tools and narrated reports directly inside your AI chat

Claude just turned its chat window into a collaborative office suite that builds functional apps and video presentations from your raw data.

November 15, 2026

  1. Deploy functional mini-apps via Whiteboards

    Instead of settling for text, ask Claude to build a Whiteboard artifact for calculators or interactive diagrams. You can leave comments on specific elements to request style changes or logic updates in real-time. This turns a simple prompt into a shareable, functional tool for your team or clients.

  2. Use Sheets for intelligent data restructuring

    Claude Sheets is ideal for cleaning messy CSVs and generating summary tabs without writing manual formulas. While it has a 100,000 cell limit, it excels at identifying patterns and automatically applying color coding based on context. This saves hours of manual prep work for small marketing datasets or customer lists.

  3. Convert raw data into narrated presentations

    Use the Visual Short and Scrollyteller features in Claude Data to turn boring tables into automated stories. The system can generate audio-visual summaries that narrate the key takeaways of your data with a real voiceover. This is a massive shortcut for creating professional status updates or client-facing reports without video editing skills.

  4. Standardize visuals with custom design systems

    Avoid the generic AI look by feeding Claude a specific design system or aesthetic preference like neo-brutalism at the start of your session. You can set specific color palettes and branding rules that the AI will follow for all generated dashboards. This ensures every artifact you share looks professional and fits your existing company identity.

Why it matters

For small business owners, the gap between gathering data and presenting it professionally usually requires expensive software or hours of manual work. These tools allow a single person to act as a data analyst, designer, and developer within one interface. It moves AI beyond simple text generation and into functional business operations.
