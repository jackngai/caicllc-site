Turn Brand PDFs Into Fully Editable Slide Decks

Move beyond static image generations and use AI to build presentations that actually let you edit the text, shapes, and data points.

August 16, 2026

  1. Clone Brand Identity via Reference Files

    Upload a previous pitch deck or a brand guidelines PDF to train the tool on your specific aesthetic. The AI extracts color schemes and layouts from these files, ensuring that new content matches your established professional identity without manual templating.

  2. Convert Static Diagrams into Editable Shapes

    If you have a complex workflow trapped in a static image or PDF, upload it to the interface. The system reconstructs the image as native PowerPoint shapes and connectors. This allows you to update specific steps or labels without recreating the entire graphic from scratch.

  3. Refine Results via Slide-Level Commenting

    Avoid broad prompts when you only need minor adjustments. Use the direct commenting feature on individual slides to request specific changes. This targeted feedback loop prevents the AI from altering parts of the presentation that are already correct.

  4. Verify Accuracy with Manual Data Overrides

    AI-generated charts often look professional but may contain placeholder numbers. Instead of regenerating the visual, access the underlying data nodes to swap in your actual metrics. This maintains the visual design while ensuring total data integrity for your stakeholders.

Why it matters

Small teams often waste hours on formatting instead of strategy. By converting static references into editable assets, you maintain a high bar for design without a full-time designer. This workflow bridges the gap between a rough outline and a client-ready presentation.
