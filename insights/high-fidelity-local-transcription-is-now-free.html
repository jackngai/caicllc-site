High Fidelity Local Transcription Is Now Free

Most transcription tools trade your privacy for accuracy and charge a monthly fee, but you can now run high quality dictation entirely on your own machine.

August 7, 2026

  1. Eliminate Privacy Risks with Local Gemma

    Google Eloquent processes audio without an internet connection by leveraging Gemma 2B or 12B models directly on your Mac. This ensures that sensitive client conversations and proprietary business ideas never leave your hardware. It removes the security concerns associated with sending voice data to cloud providers for processing.

  2. Automate Audio Cleanup and Summarization

    The Voice Edit feature uses the local model to scrub filler words like ums and ahs automatically. You can prompt the app to convert spoken notes into structured bullet points or concise meeting minutes immediately after recording. This turns raw thought dumps into polished documents without manual typing or editing.

  3. Enable Voice Driven Vibe Coding

    Use the system wide direct text insertion to dictate complex instructions into terminal based tools like Claude Code or your preferred IDE. By mapping dictation to a hotkey, you can stay in a flow state and describe logic changes out loud. This significantly reduces the friction of interacting with local AI coding agents.

  4. Secure Batch Processing for Long Form Audio

    The tool handles existing audio and video files locally at high speed. A modern Mac can process an hour of audio in approximately ten minutes, producing high quality transcripts without recurring API costs. This is ideal for builders who need to index internal training or project briefings securely.

Why it matters

Small businesses often leak sensitive data through cloud productivity tools while paying monthly fees for basic services. This tool provides enterprise grade privacy and speed at zero marginal cost. It allows solo builders to move from spoken concepts to structured data fast enough to keep up with the speed of development.
