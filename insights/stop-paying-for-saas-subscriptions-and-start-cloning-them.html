Stop Paying for SaaS Subscriptions and Start Cloning Them

When token costs for building a feature-complete Excel clone drop below ten dollars, the financial logic of paying for annual software licenses disappears.

November 4, 2026

  1. Reverse the Software Calculus

    High-reasoning models have reduced the cost of complex software development to a few hours of work and a handful of tokens. For many niche business needs, the total cost to build a custom tool is now lower than a single year of a standard SaaS subscription. Focus on cloning the high-utility cores of bloated software to eliminate your monthly overhead.

  2. Practice Strategic Feature Stripping

    Professional software suites are often filled with bloat that slows down your specific workflow. Use AI to build lightweight versions of complex tools, like PDF readers or trackers, that only include the specific functions your team actually uses. This reduces security risks and eliminates the friction of navigating unnecessary interface elements.

  3. Deploy Automated Maintenance Loops

    The primary argument against custom software is the long-term cost of maintenance and security updates. You can now offset this by using agentic workflows that periodically scan your custom codebase for vulnerabilities and update dependencies. Treating software as a living, agent-maintained asset makes bespoke tools sustainable for small teams without dedicated IT staff.

  4. Rapid Prototyping via Visual Reference

    Instead of writing massive technical requirement documents, provide the AI with screenshots of an existing user interface. Models are now proficient enough to reverse-engineer functionality from visual cues and basic descriptions of data inputs. This shortcut allows a solo builder to move from a concept to a functional clone in a single afternoon.

Why it matters

Small businesses often drown in a sea of small subscriptions that add up to significant capital drain. By building internal tools tailored to their specific data and needs, they gain a competitive edge while retaining total control over their technology stack.
