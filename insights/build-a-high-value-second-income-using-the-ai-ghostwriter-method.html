Build a High-Value Second Income Using the AI Ghostwriter Method

You can build a professional content engine in four hours a week by using Claude to transform raw expertise into high-quality scripts.

August 20, 2026

  1. The Interview Me prompt structure

    Feed Claude a raw transcript of a three minute voice memo and instruct it to interview you. Answer its clarifying questions to provide the specific details and stories the model needs to write in your voice. This workflow ensures the final output is grounded in your actual experience rather than generic data.

  2. Stick to authority based niches

    Target topics where you have at least ten years of professional experience to ensure your content provides real value. Deep expertise allows you to spot nuances that generic AI can not find on its own. This makes your work harder to replicate and more attractive to high-value clients or sponsors.

  3. Batch your production workflow

    Spend one hour a month on ideation and one morning on recording to keep your schedule predictable. Handle the scripting phase in small gaps during your day, such as your commute or lunch break, by using the Claude mobile app. This prevents the content creation process from feeling like a second full-time job.

  4. Collapse multiple roles into one AI staff

    Use Claude to act as your researcher, scriptwriter, and strategist in a single conversation thread. This removes the need to hire expensive freelancers or learn complex production skills early on. You can focus entirely on providing the core ideas while the AI manages the structural heavy lifting.

Why it matters

Most builders fail to ship content because the production process is too slow. This system turns AI into a collaborator that extracts your best ideas with minimal friction. It creates a scalable marketing asset that builds authority for your business without requiring you to quit your day job.
