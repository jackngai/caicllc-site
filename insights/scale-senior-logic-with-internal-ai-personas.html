Scale Senior Logic With Internal AI Personas

Stop using AI for generic brainstorming and start using it to replicate the specific judgment of your most experienced people.

October 6, 2026

  1. Build logic-based reviewer personas

    Small firms can scale senior expertise by building AI assistants trained on the specific thinking styles of their founders. Feed the model past memos and feedback notes to create a persona that critiques junior work in your unique voice. This provides immediate guidance for staff without requiring the founder to review every single preliminary draft.

  2. Automate the research bottleneck

    AI is now proficient enough to handle market research, initial documentation, and data analysis at scale. Small teams should use agents to produce the first sixty percent of any project, leaving human staff to handle high-stakes final decisions. This allows a three person shop to maintain the output levels of a much larger organization.

  3. Prioritize reasoning correction over editing

    General models often fail on niche technical details relevant to specific trades. To get better results, spend time explaining to the model why a specific output was wrong instead of just rewriting it manually. This human in the loop feedback builds a more reliable internal tool tailored to your industry standards.

Why it matters

Small businesses often struggle with the time tax of managing and reviewing junior staff work. By turning a founder's expertise into a digital persona, the business can scale its highest value thinking without adding headcount. This transforms the owner's specialized knowledge into a reusable asset.
