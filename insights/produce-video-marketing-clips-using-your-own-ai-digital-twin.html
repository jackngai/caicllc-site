Produce Video Marketing Clips Using Your Own AI Digital Twin

New tools allow you to scan your face via smartphone and generate consistent video scenes featuring your likeness in less than fifteen minutes.

August 1, 2026

  1. Prioritize Scanning Over Prompting for Likeness

    Traditional text to video models struggle to maintain the same face across different shots. By using the mobile scanning feature in Google Flow, you create a dedicated data point that the Gemini Omni model can reference. Tagging yourself in prompts ensures the AI applies your specific features and side profiles consistently throughout a multi-shot sequence.

  2. Automate Scene Blocking with Storyboard Agents

    Small business owners often struggle with the technical side of video production, such as camera angles and lighting. Use the integrated storyboard agent to translate a simple aesthetic description into a full production plan. This agent suggests specific shots like extreme close-ups or wide reveals, which helps non-experts create more professional and varied content.

  3. Harness Background Objects for Brand Authenticity

    The model can recognize and reproduce physical items from your environment, such as specific books or wall art, during the initial scan. This capability allows you to maintain a consistent brand aesthetic across different generated scenes. It makes the AI-generated world feel like a real extension of your physical office rather than a generic digital set.

  4. Limit Expressive Range to Avoid Visual Glitches

    Current video models still struggle with high-energy human expressions like laughing or shouting, often leading to a distracting uncanny valley effect. Stick to neutral or focused movements, such as typing or talking calmly, to keep the output believable. For marketing content, focus on these controlled actions to maintain a professional look while the tech matures.

Why it matters

Video is the most effective medium for small business marketing, but production is traditionally slow and expensive. This workflow lets a solo founder produce high-quality hype videos or explainers featuring their own face without a camera crew. It enables a level of content volume and consistency that was previously impossible for small teams.
