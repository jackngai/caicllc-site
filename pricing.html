Pricing FAQ

Questions about cost

What's included in a session?

A focused working call where we diagnose the problem, pick the right tools, and set up at least one result live. You leave with written next steps you can act on immediately.

How does the introductory rate work?

The first 11 clients get the hourly session at $97 instead of $150. It's a thank-you for working with Calibrated AI early. Once those spots are filled, the standard rate applies.

Can I cancel the monthly plan?

Anytime. Monthly Coaching is month-to-month with no long-term contract. If it stops being useful, you stop paying.

What about a custom build or team training?

Those are quoted per project, because scope varies a lot. The best path is a short conversation so we can give you a real number, not a placeholder. Get in touch and we'll sort it out.

How do I pay?

Details are sorted once we've confirmed a session fits. There's no payment required just to start the conversation.

Start with one session

If it's useful, we keep going. If not, you still leave with something working. That's the deal.
