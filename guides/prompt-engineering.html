Prompt Engineering

Level 1, the foundations. Get far better answers out of ChatGPT and Claude, starting from your very first prompts. No coding required.

Updated September 13, 2026

This guide is for people who use AI in a chat box and want better results without learning to code. You will learn the basic habits of delegation and structure that separate experts from casual users. By the end, you will know how to turn the AI into a reliable assistant for your daily research and writing.
