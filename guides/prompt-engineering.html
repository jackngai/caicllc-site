Prompt Engineering

Level 1, the foundations. Get far better answers out of ChatGPT and Claude, starting from your very first prompts. No coding required.

Updated June 28, 2026

This Level 1 guide is for people who have just started using ChatGPT or Claude in the chat box and want noticeably better answers without learning technical jargon. After reading it, they will know how to give clearer instructions, improve weak answers, check important claims, and save useful prompts for repeated work.

Who this is for: You use ChatGPT or Claude in the chat box and want noticeably better answers. No coding needed.

In this guide

Ask Better by Giving the AI a Job, Context, and Limits

A better prompt is not a clever sentence, it is a small work order: give the AI a job, the background it needs, the limits it must respect, and permission to ask before it guesses.

Read the deep dive

Make the AI Interview You Before Hard Tasks

For difficult prompts, ask the AI to question you first so it can find the missing context before it writes the thing you actually need.

Read the deep dive

Use a First Draft, Critique, Rewrite Loop

A better prompt is often not one perfect instruction, but a small editing loop: draft, critique from a real reader’s view, then rewrite the parts that failed.

Read the deep dive

Check Important Answers Before You Trust Them

Treat important AI answers as drafts that need simple checks, not as final truth, especially when money, health, reputation, or time is on the line.

Read the deep dive

Turn One Good Input Into Many Useful Outputs

A strong source document can become a whole set of useful drafts if you ask the AI to preserve the facts, change the format, and check the result before you use it.

Read the deep dive

Save Your Best Prompts as Personal Recipes

When a chat finally gives you a useful result, turn the path that got you there into a reusable recipe so your next prompt starts stronger.
