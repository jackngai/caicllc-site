Prompt Engineering

Level 1, the foundations. Get far better answers out of ChatGPT and Claude, starting from your very first prompts. No coding required.

Updated June 25, 2026

This Level 1 guide is for people who have just started using ChatGPT or Claude in a chat box and want better answers without learning technical tools. After reading it, they will know how to give clear instructions, improve weak answers, check important claims, and save prompts they can reuse for everyday work.

Who this is for: You use ChatGPT or Claude in the chat box and want noticeably better answers. No coding needed.

In this guide

Ask Better by Giving the AI a Job, Context, Limits, and a Clear Request

Better prompts come from treating the AI like a capable helper who needs a job title, useful background, clear boundaries, and one specific next step.

Read the deep dive

Use Clarifying Questions Before You Ask for the Final Answer

When a request is fuzzy, ask the AI to question you first so the final answer is based on the real situation, not its best guess.

Read the deep dive

Draft in Two Steps: First the Plan, Then the Finished Work

For better AI drafts, stop asking for the finished piece first: ask for the plan, fix the plan, then ask the AI to write from that approved structure.

Read the deep dive

Make the AI Improve Its Own Answer With a Specific Critic

A better answer often comes from asking the AI to criticize its draft as a specific real reader, then using that criticism as an editing checklist.

Read the deep dive

Check Important Answers Before You Trust Them

When accuracy matters, treat an AI answer as a draft to inspect, not a verdict to accept.

Read the deep dive

Turn Good Chats Into Reusable Prompts

The fastest way to get better results is to turn chats that worked into saved prompts, then improve them every time they fail.
