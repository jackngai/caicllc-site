Prompt Engineering

Level 1, the foundations. Get far better answers out of ChatGPT and Claude, starting from your very first prompts. No coding required.

Updated July 12, 2026

This Level 1 guide is for people who have just started using ChatGPT or Claude in the chat box and want better answers without learning code. After reading it, they will know how to give clearer instructions, ask for missing-context questions, check important answers, and turn useful prompts into repeatable habits.

Who this is for: You use ChatGPT or Claude in the chat box and want noticeably better answers. No coding needed.

In this guide

Write Prompts That Give the AI Enough to Work With

Better AI answers come from giving the chat box four things in order: the task, the background, the limits, and the kind of response you want.

Read the deep dive

Ask the AI to Interview You Before It Answers

Before you ask for a finished answer, ask the AI to ask you one useful question at a time so it can understand the job, the reader, the limits, and the missing facts.

Read the deep dive

Make the AI Show Its Work as a Plan First

Ask the AI for a plan before the answer when the task has enough moving parts that a wrong assumption would waste your time.

Read the deep dive

Check Important Answers Without Becoming an Expert

Treat AI answers as drafts to inspect, not facts to accept, using a simple check loop that fits everyday work.

Read the deep dive

Use AI for Repeatable Everyday Workflows

The fastest way to get better everyday AI results is to stop starting from scratch and turn repeat tasks into small, reusable routines with clear checks.

Know When to Keep Control Instead of Automating

Better prompting starts with deciding what the AI may do alone, what needs your judgment, and when it must stop and ask.
