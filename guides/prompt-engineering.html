Prompt Engineering

Level 1, the foundations. Get far better answers out of ChatGPT and Claude, starting from your very first prompts. No coding required.

Updated July 19, 2026

This Level 1 guide is for people who have just started using ChatGPT or Claude in the chat box and want better answers for everyday work. You will learn how to give clearer instructions, add useful context, ask for the right format, and check important answers before using them.

Who this is for: You use ChatGPT or Claude in the chat box and want noticeably better answers. No coding needed.

In this guide

Write Prompts That Include the Job, Context, Limits, and Next Ask

A good prompt is not a clever script, but a simple handoff: name the job, give the situation, set the boundaries, then ask what is needed next.

Read the deep dive

Make the AI Ask You Questions Before It Answers

A short instruction to ask clarifying questions turns AI from a guesser into a careful assistant, especially when your request depends on details you have not typed yet.

Read the deep dive

Use Roles and Examples to Get Answers That Fit Your Situation

Generic prompts get generic answers; roles, audience details, and examples give the AI a target it can actually hit.

Read the deep dive

Ask for a Specific Output You Can Actually Use

Better AI answers start with naming the thing you need next: a table, checklist, draft, comparison, timeline, or simple visual that is already shaped for action.

Read the deep dive

Break Big Requests Into Research, Outline, Draft, and Review

For large requests, get better results by asking the AI to research, organize, draft, and critique in separate passes instead of demanding a finished answer all at once.

Read the deep dive

Check Important Answers Before You Trust Them

Treat important AI answers as drafts to check, not facts to accept, using simple prompts that expose missing context, uncertainty, and disagreement.
