Use the Interview Method to Capture Your Voice

Stop struggling to sound human by turning your AI into a professional interviewer that extracts your knowledge and personality before it writes a single word.

Updated September 6, 2026

The Problem with Generic Prompts

Most beginners treat ChatGPT or Claude like a search engine or a magic button. You type a short request and hope the machine guesses your intent. The result is usually AI-speak, which is polite, wordy, and boring. This happens because the AI does not know your specific experiences or how you talk. It relies on the average of everything it has read on the internet, which is the definition of generic.

To get better results, you must change the relationship. Instead of being a boss giving a vague order, act like an expert being interviewed. By providing a small amount of raw, unpolished thought and then letting the AI ask you questions, you bridge the gap between robotic text and your actual voice. This process turns the software into a ghostwriter, a professional writer who writes for someone else, that captures your authority.

Provide the Raw Material

Start by getting your ideas out of your head without worrying about grammar or structure. You can use the voice-to-text feature on your phone to record a two or three minute talk about your topic. If you prefer typing, write a messy list of bullet points. The goal is to provide raw data that contains your unique perspective and professional knowledge. This raw input is the foundation that keeps the output from sounding like everyone else.

Once you have this text, paste it into the chat box with a specific instruction. Tell the AI that this is a rough transcript and you want it to help you expand it. Do not ask it to write the final version yet. Simply state that you are the expert and it is the assistant. This setup prevents the AI from jumping to conclusions and forces it to listen to your specific input first.

The Interview Loop

The most effective way to refine the output is to tell the AI to interview you. Use a prompt like this: Based on the notes I provided, ask me five clarifying questions that will help you write this in my voice. This step is vital because the AI will spot the gaps in your logic or the details you forgot to mention. It might ask for a specific example, a story from your career, or how you feel about a certain trend.

When you answer these questions, you are giving the AI the high-value details that separate an expert from an amateur. You do not need to write perfect sentences in your response. Just provide the facts. This interaction teaches the AI your specific vocabulary and the way you prioritize information. It moves the process from a one-way command to a two-way conversation that builds a much stronger final product.

Refining the Final Result

After the interview is complete, tell the AI to produce the final document using your original notes and your answers to the questions. Because the AI now has a deeper understanding of your point of view, the resulting email, article, or plan will sound authentic. It will use your examples and follow your logic rather than relying on overused phrases. You have essentially automated the role of a researcher and a writer.

If the first draft still feels a bit too formal, you can ask for one final adjustment. Tell it to make the tone more conversational or remove the fluff. By using this interview method, you spend less time editing bad drafts and more time sharing your actual expertise. You move from being a frustrated user to a confident director of your digital staff.

Key takeaways

  • Start with a raw voice transcript or a messy brain dump to give the AI your unique perspective.
  • Command the AI to ask you clarifying questions before it attempts to write a final draft.
  • Use your professional experience to answer the AI questions, which provides the high-value detail generic prompts lack.
  • Treat the AI as a ghostwriter that needs your specific input to produce human-sounding results.
  • Avoid the one-shot habit where you expect a perfect result from a single, short sentence.
