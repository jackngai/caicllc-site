The Interview Technique for Better Writing

Stop struggling with perfect prompts and let the AI interview you to capture your unique expertise and personal voice.

Updated November 1, 2026

The Problem with Generic Output

When you ask an AI to write an email or a report, it often produces text that feels stiff, overly formal, or generic. This happens because the software is trying to predict the most average response based on everything it has read on the internet. It does not know your specific history, your unique perspective, or the nuances of your current situation. Most people try to fix this by writing longer and more complex prompts, which is exhausting and often fails.

To get better results, you must bridge the gap between the knowledge in your head and the empty chat box. If you do not provide your own specific context, the AI will fill in the blanks with clichés. The most effective way to provide this context is not by writing a perfect paragraph of instructions, but by allowing the AI to pull the information out of you through a conversation.

Using Agent-Led Discovery

The secret to better writing is a concept called agent-led discovery. This simply means you ask the AI to play the role of an interviewer. Instead of giving a command and hoping for the best, you tell the AI what you want to achieve and then ask it to interview you. For example, if you need to write a project summary, you could say: I need to write a summary of my last month of work for my manager. Before you write anything, ask me five questions to help you understand my accomplishments and the tone I should use.

This shift in roles removes the pressure on you to remember every detail. The AI becomes a researcher that extracts the necessary information from you. By answering these targeted questions, you provide the software with a source of truth. This ensures the final draft is grounded in your actual work rather than generic guesses. It turns the AI into a partner that helps you define your intent before it starts the work of drafting.

Capturing Your Unique Voice

You can make this process even faster by providing raw, messy input like a voice transcript or a list of bullet points. If you have ten years of experience in a field, you have insights that a machine cannot invent. You can record a two-minute voice memo of yourself talking naturally about a topic and paste the transcript into the chat. Tell the AI to analyze your thoughts and ask clarifying questions about any points that seem incomplete or vague.

This method acts like a ghostwriter for your brain. It captures the way you speak and the specific authority you bring to a subject. Because the AI is working from your raw thoughts, the resulting text will sound like you, just polished and organized. This is especially helpful for professionals who have deep knowledge but find the actual process of writing to be a bottleneck in their day.

Making the Interview a Habit

To master this technique, make it your default habit for any complex task. Whenever you feel stuck or find yourself deleting and rewriting AI responses, stop and ask the machine to interview you. This iterative process allows you to correct the AI's understanding before it generates a full page of text. It saves time because you spend your energy on sharing your expertise rather than fixing bad drafts.

By moving from a model where you give orders to a model where you answer questions, you ensure your work stands out. The end result is a piece of writing that reflects your true expertise and professional voice. You are no longer just a user of AI; you are the director of a process that turns your personal knowledge into high-quality communication.

Key takeaways

  • Start your prompt by asking the AI to interview you with five to ten specific questions.
  • Use raw voice transcripts or messy notes as a source of truth for the AI to analyze.
  • Focus on your areas of deep expertise to ensure the output is high-value and unique.
  • Use the interview to define your intent and goals clearly before any drafting begins.
  • Correct the AI during the conversation if its questions show it has misunderstood your tone.
