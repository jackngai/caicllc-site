The Interview Technique for Better Writing

Learn how to transform a messy voice recording into high-quality writing by letting the AI interview you to capture your unique expertise.

Updated November 8, 2026

Start with a Conversation, Not a Blank Page

Most people struggle with a blank document because they try to write and edit at the same time. The best way to capture your knowledge is to talk about it. Take your phone, open a recording app, and spend three minutes explaining your topic as if you were talking to a friend. Do not worry about being perfect or using formal grammar. Just focus on your core ideas and the specific details only you know from your years of work experience.

Once you have the recording, use a transcription tool or the voice-to-text feature on your phone to get the text. This messy transcript is your raw material. It contains your natural personality and unique insights that a standard typed prompt would miss. By starting with your own voice, you ensure the final result is grounded in your actual perspective rather than generic internet data.

The Interview Prompt

After you paste your transcript into the chat box, do not ask the AI to write an article yet. If you do, the AI will fill in the gaps with its own generic style. Instead, tell the AI that it is acting as your ghostwriter. Ask it to read the transcript and then list five questions it needs answered to make the writing sound more like you. This turns the AI into a collaborator that pulls more information out of your head.

This step is important because it stops the AI from guessing. When the AI asks you questions, you can respond with short, informal sentences. This second round of information provides the connective tissue for your writing. It allows the tool to understand your specific goals and the nuances of your professional background, making the eventual draft feel authentic and authoritative.

Building a Consistent Digital Persona

To get even better results, you can treat the AI like an actor that needs to learn a role. You can provide a set of instructions that describe your tone, your favorite phrases, and the things you never say. Think of this as a set of rules that keeps the AI from drifting into robotic language. You are essentially creating a knowledge base for the AI so it knows exactly how to represent you.

Even the most advanced AI can sometimes get stuck or sound too formal. If the draft it produces feels off, tell it exactly which parts sound fake. You can say, "I would never use these specific words" or "Make this sound more like a direct email to a client." Over time, the AI learns your preferences and acts as a better reflection of your own professional identity. This consistency is what makes your writing stand out from standard AI output.

Moving from One Piece to a System

The real power of this technique comes when you stop doing it one piece at a time. Instead of recording one memo when you need to write, try a method called batching. Spend thirty minutes once a week recording four or five short memos on different topics. This allows you to stay in a creative flow and avoids the stress of trying to come up with ideas on the spot for every single task.

By setting aside specific times for talking and specific times for prompting, you can handle the work of a researcher and a writer in a fraction of the time. This workflow allows you to maintain a high volume of professional content without feeling overwhelmed. You provide the expertise and the voice, while the AI handles the heavy lifting of organization and formatting. This resolves the problem of the blank page and lets you focus on being the expert.

Key takeaways

  • Record a three-minute voice memo to capture your natural tone and expert knowledge before you start writing.
  • Ask the AI to interview you by requesting five clarifying questions about your transcript before it generates a draft.
  • Define specific rules for your digital persona by telling the AI which words to avoid and what tone to maintain.
  • Batch your recordings by talking through several topics in one session to save time and stay in a productive mindset.
  • Focus your content on areas where you have at least ten years of experience to ensure the AI's output is high-value and unique.
