Save Effort with the Interview Method

Stop struggling to write the perfect prompt and start letting the AI interview you to get the results you actually want.

Updated August 23, 2026

The Problem of the Blank Page

Most people approach AI like a search engine or a vending machine. They type a short, vague request and hope for a miracle. When the result is generic or misses the mark, they assume the tool is not smart enough for the job. The reality is that the AI is often guessing what you want because it lacks context. Writing a massive, detailed paragraph to fix this is exhausting and often leads to more confusion for the machine.

Instead of doing the heavy lifting yourself, let the AI act as a consultant. This is the Interview Method. You provide a broad goal and then ask the AI to question you until it has everything it needs. This flips the script from you being a writer to you being a subject matter expert. It ensures the final output matches your specific situation rather than a generic template.

The Power of the Meta-Prompt

To start an interview, you need a simple habit known as a meta-prompt. This is just a prompt about the prompt itself. Instead of asking for a finished email or project plan immediately, tell the AI your task and then say: "Ask me ten clarifying questions before you start writing so you have all the context you need." This forces the tool to identify its own gaps in knowledge before it begins the work.

You can make this even more effective by following a simple structure. State your task, give a little background information, and list any constraints like your preferred length or tone. By ending your request with a question about what the AI needs, you prevent it from hallucinating. Hallucination is when an AI fills in missing information with made-up facts. When you let the AI interview you, you provide the facts so it does not have to invent them.

Using Your Voice to Save Time

One of the biggest hurdles to giving the AI good information is the time it takes to type. If you are trying to write a complex article or a weekly schedule, typing every detail feels like doing the work twice. To bypass this, use the voice-to-text feature on your phone. Speak your thoughts naturally for a few minutes, including all the messy details, side notes, and even things you are not sure about yet.

Upload that rough transcript and tell the AI to treat it as raw material. Ask the AI to organize those thoughts and then interview you to fill in any missing pieces. This method captures your unique voice and professional expertise far better than a typed summary ever could. It allows you to move from a rough idea to a polished draft without ever staring at a blinking cursor.

Reviewing the Plan Before the Finish

Once the AI has interviewed you and gathered the facts, ask it to create a plan of action or an outline before it generates the final result. This is like a chef showing you a recipe before they start cooking. If the plan looks right, you give the green light. If something is off, you can correct it before the AI spends effort on the final output. This layer of verification ensures the final product meets your standards.

This habit of reviewing a plan first saves you from the frustration of results that are almost right but not quite there. If the AI learns that you prefer a specific professional tone or certain formatting, you can tell it to remember those preferences as skills for next time. By treating the AI as an assistant that needs a clear brief, you build a repeatable process that gets better with every conversation. You stop working for the AI and start making the AI work for you.

Key takeaways

  • End your initial prompts by asking the AI what additional information it needs to provide a high-quality answer.
  • Use the TICA framework: define the Task, provide Information, set Constraints, and Ask for clarifying questions.
  • Provide raw context quickly by speaking into a voice-to-text tool rather than typing long explanations.
  • Request an outline or implementation plan to verify the AI's logic before it generates the final response.
  • Prevent hallucinations by providing the specific facts the AI identifies as missing during the interview process.
