Delegating Travel and Research Tasks

Learn how to transform your AI into a tireless assistant that filters complex data and presents you with the best decisions for travel and projects.

Updated November 15, 2026

Creating a Dedicated Research Space

Instead of asking the AI a general question like "where should I fly," start by giving it a specific frame of reference. This means uploading screenshots of flight searches or pasting in lists of your frequent flyer programs, such as Aeroplan or Flying Blue. By giving the AI your specific constraints first, you prevent it from giving you generic advice that does not apply to your actual situation.

Think of this as training a new employee. You would not tell a person to find a flight without telling them your budget and which websites to check. When you use flight search tools, take a screenshot of the results and paste it into the chat. Ask the AI to look for verified availability to ensure the flight is actually bookable. This prevents the common mistake of the AI suggesting a flight that no longer exists.

Finding the Best Value Through Comparison

Once the AI has your data, its most valuable job is performing the math you do not want to do. For travel, this involves calculating if buying airline points during a sale is cheaper than paying cash. You can ask the AI to find the deal floor, which is the minimum price point where a luxury flight becomes a bargain. This same logic works for market research by asking the AI to compare the price of an item against its historical average.

The failure mode here is trusting the AI with numbers without a double check. Always ask the AI to show its work by listing the math it used to reach a recommendation. If you are researching a product to sell on a site like Etsy, have the AI analyze search volume against competition levels. This ensures you are making a decision based on hard data rather than a hunch.

Turning Research Into Action

High quality research is useless if it stays inside the chat box. You can close the loop by asking the AI to format its findings into a clear list of next steps. For example, if it finds a great flight deal, tell it to write a checklist of the three things you need to do to book this right now. This moves the project from a conversation into a concrete plan.

You can even use this for your daily tasks or professional meetings. Paste a transcript from a meeting or a long thread of emails into the AI and ask it to extract the action items. This keeps you in the driver's seat by letting you review a summary instead of digging through raw data. By treating the AI as a filter, you save hours of manual sorting and focus only on the final execution.

Key takeaways

  • Always provide the AI with specific data sources like screenshots or program lists rather than asking open-ended questions.
  • Ask the AI to calculate the deal floor by comparing the cost of points or discounts against the standard cash price.
  • Request that the AI show its work to ensure the mathematical calculations for your research are accurate.
  • Use the AI to filter long emails or transcripts into a simple list of action items to save time on follow-ups.
  • Verify seat or product availability manually before making a final purchase based on AI suggestions.
